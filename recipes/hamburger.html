Everyone loves a good Burger

This is one dish I make quite often, at least once a week. Like all burgers, you can whip up a homemade hamburger in under 30 minutes, which makes it a family favorite on busy days. This is my kids’ go-to burger along with my chicken burger, cheeseburger, and smash burger.

Ingredients

  • 1 pound ground beef (80/20 blend recommended for juiciness)
  • 4 burger buns, sliced
  • 4 slices of cheddar or American cheese
  • Lettuce, tomato, onion slices, and pickles (optional toppings)
  • Salt, pepper, and garlic powder (for seasoning)
  • Butter or oil for toasting buns
  • Your favorite burger sauce or condiments (ketchup, mustard, mayo, etc.)

Steps

  1. Start by making the burger patties using ground beef, seasoned with salt, pepper, and garlic powder. Form into 4 even patties and let them rest while you prep the rest.
  2. While the patties rest, slice your lettuce, tomatoes, and onions. Get your cheese, sauces, and buns ready so everything’s within reach when it’s time to assemble.
  3. Grill or pan-fry the patties over medium-high heat until nicely seared and cooked through. Add a slice of cheese on top of each patty during the last minute of cooking to let it melt.
  4. Toast the buns with a bit of butter, then build your burger: sauce on the bottom bun, patty with melted cheese, toppings of your choice, then the top bun. Serve hot and enjoy!
